## Contribution checklist

<!-- DOOZCTL:BEGIN:v1 contribution -->

- Hard gates: every explicit gate needs direct evidence — tests executed (not just present), `doozctl verify` READY when a plan exists, `doozctl doctor` healthy, build/typecheck/lint green.
- Definition of Done: scope complete, acceptance satisfied, verification observed, final diff reviewed, no TODO/stub/debug left. If any gate lacks evidence, do not declare DONE.
- Review: confirm no regressions or hidden failures, no scope bloat; brief red-team for correctness/security/race/complexity proportional to risk.

<!-- DOOZCTL:END:v1 contribution -->
